# Ideal Customer Profile

**Name (invented) and one-line sketch:** Shota, 56, runs a mid-sized construction company building residential projects in Tbilisi and Kutaisi.

**Their situation:** He has 30 years in construction and runs several sites at once. He hires a labor safety specialist who visits each site once every 4–5 days. Between visits, safety depends on foremen and workers. He coordinates by phone and WhatsApp.

**The moment the problem shows up:** On the days between the safety specialist's visits, when workers skip helmets they have but find uncomfortable, dump debris outside the designated area, and leave slab edges without guardrails after removing them to lift materials, and nobody reports it until a labor inspector arrives.

**What it costs them:** A 5,000 GEL fine and a 14-day work stoppage on the "Mwvanekvavila" project for missing PPE, a violation that had already been spotted and never fixed. Across a company, repeated violations add up: about 150,000 GEL in fines over 4 months (Interview 2). A stoppage costs more than the fine: up to 5,000 GEL a day in delay penalties from the developer (Interview 4).

**What they do about it today:** Relies on the specialist's periodic visits and on foremen's word; violations noted on one visit are not tracked until the next. Phones site engineers to check before audits (Interview 4).

**What they are NOT:**
- People building their own private house: they hire workers directly, have no safety specialist, and are rarely inspected.
- Small renovation crews working inside apartments: no slabs or heights, rarely inspected, no safety specialist to work around.
