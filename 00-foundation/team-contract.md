## How we work

- **Channel:** "TEMPO" WhatsApp group
- **Standup:** Monday 20:00–20:15, F7 auditorium or Google Meet
- **Response window:** messages acknowledged within 3 hours between 09:00 and 21:00
- **Silent teammate with a deadline near:** Tekla follows up by direct message after 5 hours of silence; if no reply within 24 hours, she calls, and the rest of the team splits the work and notes it in the channel
- **Demo-ready:** any member can screen-share the current product state in any lab, on 2 minutes notice
- **Code changes:** nobody pushes directly to `main`; every change goes through a pull request approved by at least one other member

## How we decide

Small stuff: any two members. Big stuff and direction: we meet, look at the evidence first, then discuss it and aim for unanimous agreement. If we can't all agree, majority decides and the dissent gets one sentence in the record. From Week 3, product decisions go in `DECISIONS.md` with a link to their evidence. That file is the team memory; keep it honest.

## When it breaks

1. Name it in the team channel as soon as you notice it, and no later than 24 hours. Silence is how last year's teams collapsed.
2. Still stuck: we hold a meeting (in person or Google Meet) within 24 hours. If that doesn't solve it, raise it at the next lab checkpoint.
3. Involves everyone, or step 2 failed: email zeshan.ahmad@kiu.edu.ge for mediation.
