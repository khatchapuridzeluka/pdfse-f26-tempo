## The strongest incumbent

The visiting safety specialist, with the engineer's or foreman's word covering the days in between (rows 1, 4, 6, 7). Every company with a specialist relies on it (Logs 1, 2, 3, 4), nobody wants to drop the specialist, and the complaints are all about the days he is not there: "Until the next visit, monitoring that issue relies solely on my verbal oversight." (Log 1) Anything we test has to work on the days between visits, not replace the specialist.

---

## Explicitly not used

- **No automated tool or system:** the owner said nothing is automated. (Log 2)
- **No written record between visits:** the specialist gives feedback only verbally (Log 1), and a small crew fills in no paperwork or checklist (Log 7).
