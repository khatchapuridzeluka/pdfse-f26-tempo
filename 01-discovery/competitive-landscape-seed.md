## Workarounds and alternatives mentioned

| # | Workaround / alternative | What they do with it | Why it falls short | Log |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Paid safety specialist visiting every 4 days** | The specialist comes to the site once every 4 days and tells workers what not to violate. | Covers one day in four; violations happen in between. | 2 |
| 2 | **The specialist's checklist** | After each visit, the specialist leaves a checklist of what should be done. | Nobody makes sure it is followed between visits. | 2 |
| 3 | **Owner being strict with workers** | The owner tries to be strict so the rules are followed. | Workers still repeat the same violations; ~150,000 GEL in fines in 4 months. | 2 |
| 4 | **The specialist's own attention on site** | He watches for violations himself when he is there. | He can't be on site 24 hours a day or watch for simple violations every moment. | 3 |
| 5 | **The labor inspector (unintended)** | In practice, violations are found by the labor inspector, not by the team. | It finds problems only by fining or warning the company. | 2 |
