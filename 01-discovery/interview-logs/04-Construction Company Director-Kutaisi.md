# Interview 04 · Founder & Managing Director (Construction Company) · L.G.

**Week:** 3 · **Asker:** TT · **Logger:** GG · **Consent:** yes, notes and audio recording confirmed (However, we could not record audio as the talk happened over a phone call)
**Real or practice:** real

**Context:** Founder and Managing Director (L.G) of a mid-sized residential construction firm in Kutaisi. Directly oversees regulatory compliance, subcontractor agreements, and site project managers.

**Our prediction:** We are wrong if company founders tell us that state safety fines are small enough to be treated as normal business overhead, or that they have direct digital visibility into daily site-level hazard fixes without calling their site engineers.

**Workaround:** Holds weekly face-to-face syncs with site engineers and safety officers; relies on executive directors making random physical site visits to catch violations
**Cost:** GEL 15,000+ lost in project delay penalties last year. 4 hours per week wasted making manual phone calls to site engineers to verify compliance before state audits
**Frequency:** State Labour Inspection audits occur unannounced 1–2 times a year per site. Executive site visits occur once every 2 weeks.
**Prediction check:** Yes (confirmed that state fines trigger far costlier project delay penalties, and founders lack any real-time dashboard or record of site safety).
**Surprise ★:** Founders also lose money because of the inspector fines, as they might have to stop working.
