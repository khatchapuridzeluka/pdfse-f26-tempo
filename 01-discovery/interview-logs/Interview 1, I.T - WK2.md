**Workaround:** Gives verbal reminders in the morning during task handoffs and relies on ongoing phone call reminders during active construction phases.
**Cost:** Time spent filling out compliance reports and resolving inspector-mandated grace periods; potential financial penalties handled at executive level.
**Frequency:** Inspectors visit approximately twice a year unannounced, though company safety specialists only visit once every 4–5 days or once a week.
**Prediction check:** Yes (raised the struggle of maintaining worker PPE compliance during daily engineering tasks without constant supervision before we brought it up).
**Surprise ★:** Site engineers focus almost exclusively on technical/structural execution and view safety enforcement as an auxiliary distraction from daily build schedules; they often do not track fine amounts directly as that rests with executive directors.
**Commitment:** Introduction to partner construction safety inspector / site manager; agreed to follow up when we have a low-fidelity prototype.
**Next:** Interview their safety specialist (Levan) and ask about formal checklist handoffs and daily monitoring protocols between visits.
