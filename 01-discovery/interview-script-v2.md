# Interview Script v2

Problem: construction site safety compliance and PPE violations in Georgia

## Prediction

> Wk3 · Prediction "We are wrong if site managers tell us that workers maintain full PPE compliance without oversight, or that fines from unannounced inspections are rare or negligible risks they actively ignore." verdict: confirmed · evidence: Interview 1, Interview 2, Interview 4

## Kept from v1

- Last time an inspector came (every interview gave a real recent case)
- Last time a rule was broken and how it was found
- What it cost (owners and founders gave numbers, workers did not)
- What they do when the safety specialist is not there
- A short question about a normal workday, as a warm-up

## Cut from v1

| Question | Why it is cut |
|---|---|
| Is PPE missing or is it a habit? | Same answer in Logs 2 and 3: PPE is available, workers just do not wear it. |
| Does the specialist leave a checklist? | Yes/no question. We want to know what happens to the checklist, so it is replaced by new question 6. |

## New in v2

| New question | Surprise it came from | Logs |
|---|---|---|
| Who dealt with the last fine or stoppage? | Owners and directors pay the cost, not the people on site | 1, 2, 4 |
| How did you find out about PPE on a day you were not on site? | Nobody above the foremen sees daily compliance until an inspector comes | 1, 2, 4 |
| Show me the last safety checklist or log. When was it filled in? | Safety records are verbal or filled in by hand at the last minute | 1, 2, 4 |
| What happened to the person after the last violation? | A worker was blamed as an individual and the company was not fined (one log only, so not confirmed yet) | 3 |

## The script

1. Walk me through the last full day you spent on one of your sites.
2. Tell me about the last time an inspector or safety specialist came to your site. What happened that day?
3. Tell me about the last time safety rules were not followed. How did you find out? What happened to the person involved?
4. What did that cost you (fine, days stopped, penalties)? Who dealt with it?
5. Think of the last day you were not on site. How did you find out whether workers wore their PPE?
6. Show me the last safety checklist or log that was filled in. When was it filled in, and who looked at it after?
7. Who else should I talk to about this?
