## Step 2: The hypothesis we test first
> **We believe** Construction company owners and site managers in Georgia running active building projects
> **struggle with** maintaining worker PPE compliance and site safety standards on days the safety specialist is not present,
> **when** an unexpected labor inspector visits the active site unannounced,
> **which costs them** thousands of GEL in unexpected regulatory fines and multi-week project stoppages (e.g., 14 days of downtime).
> **Today they** pay a labor safety specialist to visit the site physically once every 4–5 days and rely on informal verbal reminders from site foremen on off-days.
> **We are wrong if** site managers tell us that workers maintain full PPE compliance without oversight, or that fines from unannounced inspections are rare/negligible risks they actively ignore.
