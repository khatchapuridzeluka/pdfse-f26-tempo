## Week 3 · Kill your favorite idea

- **Applied the pattern:** My belief in the problem hypothesis was that "workers skip essential PPE (helmets, guardrails)" on days the specialist isn't on site. Half of it died: PPE is on site, and the owner said workers skip it because it is uncomfortable, not because it is missing (02). The guardrail part got stronger, but for a different reason than I thought: the specialist named edge fences as the most common violation, removed while lifting materials and not put back (03), and the team lead described his own crew doing exactly that (05). So the problem is not missing safety, it is safety that gets undone during normal work and nobody notices.
- **Agency beyond assigned lane:** Reviewed a teammate's Script v2 and flagged citations that did not match the logs.
- **Hacked faster:** Instead of searching for a safety specialist, I asked the owner at the end of interview 02 who else we should talk to. He gave me his specialist's phone number directly, and I interviewed him two days later (03).
